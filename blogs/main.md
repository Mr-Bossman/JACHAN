# About me
### Hi! 👋 My name is Jesse Taube

I am yet another C coder (YACC).

I attend the University of Massachusetts Lowell, pursuing a degree in Computer Engineering. I spend most of my free time experimenting with electronics and software, and also maintaining several servers that host this site. My servers run on Debian and are being managed by Cockpit and KVM. I also use them to gain a working knowledge of enterprise-grade networking. To complement that, I am also learning Cisco's IOS command line interface on a Cisco router and switch.

I am knowledgeable in C and C++ with a bit of experience in JS, Python, Java, Swift, and Bash.
I treasure all different types of embedded projects, having started learning with Arduino when I was 8,
but as of late, I have picked up learning how other MCUs work with great zeal. I'd still probably have to say my favorite MCUs are the Attiny series. Recently, I have been tinkering my way into embedded Linux and FreeRTOS. Which has landed me with quite a few [contributions to the Linux Kernel](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commits?author=Mr-Bossman&since=2021-11-01), and my internships at [D3 Embedded](https://d3embedded.com), and [Rivos Inc](https://www.rivosinc.com).

Because of my experience working with the Linux kernel, C has become my favorite language to use. With that, I have come to quite like the Kernel's coding style, with the exception of braces of function definitions being on the same line as they should be.

#### Personal life
As I live in the greater Boston area, I have the luxury of being near MIT and many tech companies. MIT hosts the [MIT Flea](https://w1mx.mit.edu/flea-at-mit/), which is a great place to socialize and buy old electronics. RIP DEC.

Recently, I have also been going to raves. I have been listening to Happy Hardcore for most of my life, and it's been an enlightening experience finding friends to dance with to my favorite genre. Going to the free raves has also allowed me to take [stunning photos](https://gallery.jachan.dev) of the party-goers and DJs

If you ever want to find me, I will most likely be at the MIT Flea, the free raves, or any Happy Hardcore shows in Boston or Providence. You can also message me as I'm always ready for an adventure.
